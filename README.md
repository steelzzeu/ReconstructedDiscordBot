
**🤖 ReconstructedAI - Your Customizable Discord Bot**

**🌟 About ReconstructedAI**
ReconstructedAI is an open-source Discord bot designed to enhance your server's functionality. With a focus on customization, it allows you to tailor its features to suit your community's unique needs.

**Key Features:**

🎮 Interactive Commands: Engage your community with fun and interactive commands.
🛡️ Moderation Tools: Maintain a safe environment with robust moderation capabilities.
⚙️ Customizable Settings: Easily adjust configurations via the config.json file to fit your server's requirements.
